💹 Am I beating Bet365?

After each match HAWK compares the Bet365 price you got on each leg with Bet365's last price before kick-off (the "closing price"). If you keep getting better prices than the close, you're betting before the market moves your way — the surest early sign of a real edge, long before wins and losses even out.
