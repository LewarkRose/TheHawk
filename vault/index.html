🗓️Weekly report

📅By week

By match day, not settle day — the night a ticket was played is the week it belongs to, which is the same rule the report above and the rows below use. The bar is won against lost; the figures are tickets, strike rate and return on what you staked.

📈Profit over time

Every settled bet in order, oldest to newest. A cash-out counts what came back, not what the ticket would have returned.

🌍By competition

The bar is return either side of break-even. Competitions with fewer than three settled bets are left out — at two bets a number says nothing.

⚖️How good is HAWK

🎯Does 60% really mean 60%?

Each row takes every leg HAWK gave roughly the same chance, then checks how often they actually landed. It saves those chances before kick-off and marks them afterwards, so it fills up whether you bet or not.

💰Bankroll

Every change is written to the log below with what it was, what it became and why. A sync carries these across, which is how money you moved by hand on one device reaches the other — a ticket's own stake and return travel with the ticket.
